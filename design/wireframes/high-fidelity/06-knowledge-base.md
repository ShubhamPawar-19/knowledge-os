# Knowledge Base

## 1. Purpose

The Knowledge Base serves as the centralized repository of organizational knowledge. It aggregates documents, connected data sources, indexed content, and AI-generated knowledge to provide a structured, searchable, and maintainable source of truth.

---

## 2. Primary Users

- Workspace Member
- Workspace Admin
- Organization Owner

---

## 3. User Goals

Users should be able to:

- Browse organizational knowledge
- Organize knowledge into collections
- Search knowledge
- View indexed content
- Monitor knowledge health
- Manage tags and categories
- Improve AI knowledge quality
